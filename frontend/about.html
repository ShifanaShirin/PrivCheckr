About PrivCheckr

PrivCheckr helps users understand privacy policies before giving consent by converting complex legal text into clear, actionable insights.

What is PrivCheckr?

PrivCheckr is designed to address the lack of transparency in privacy policies. Many users agree to terms without fully understanding how their personal data is collected, used, stored, or shared.

The system analyzes privacy policy text and highlights key data practices, privacy risks, and compliance indicators in a user-friendly format.

DPDP Act Awareness

The Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 is India’s data protection law that regulates how organizations process personal data. It emphasizes lawful processing, informed consent, purpose limitation, data retention, and user rights.

PrivCheckr uses simplified DPDP-inspired principles to evaluate privacy policies and present compliance insights without legal complexity.

What PrivCheckr Evaluates

  • User consent before data collection
  • Purpose limitation for data usage
  • Data storage and retention practices
  • User rights such as access, update, and deletion
  • Grievance and contact mechanisms

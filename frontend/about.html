About PrivCheckr

PrivCheckr helps users understand privacy policies before giving consent by converting complex legal text into clear, actionable insights.

What is PrivCheckr?

PrivCheckr analyzes privacy policies and highlights data practices, privacy risks, and compliance indicators in a user-friendly format.

DPDP Act Awareness

The Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 regulates how organizations process personal data in India.

What PrivCheckr Evaluates

  • User consent before data collection
  • Purpose limitation for data usage
  • Data storage and retention practices
  • User rights such as access, update, and deletion
  • Grievance mechanisms

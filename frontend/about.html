About PrivCheckr

PrivCheckr is designed to help users understand privacy policies before giving consent. Most privacy policies are written in complex legal language, making it difficult for users to know how their data is collected, used, or retained.

PrivCheckr analyzes privacy policy text and presents clear insights such as data collection practices, privacy risks, and DPDP compliance indicators. The goal is to promote transparency and informed decision-making.

This project is developed as an academic and research-oriented system, inspired by real-world privacy compliance tools.

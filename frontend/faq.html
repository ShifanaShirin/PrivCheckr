Trust & Frequently Asked Questions

Does PrivCheckr store my privacy policy text?

No. PrivCheckr analyzes the text in real time and does not store user-submitted privacy policies.

Is this legal advice?

No. PrivCheckr provides informational insights to improve understanding. It is not a substitute for professional legal advice.

Which laws does PrivCheckr consider?

PrivCheckr focuses on principles derived from India’s Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

Who can use PrivCheckr?

Students, app users, developers, researchers, and anyone who wants to understand privacy policies more clearly.

Is PrivCheckr free?

Yes. PrivCheckr is currently provided as an academic project and research tool.

Trust & Frequently Asked Questions

Does PrivCheckr store my privacy policy text?

PrivCheckr stores analysis results securely for logged-in users to allow viewing past analyses. Policy text is not shared with third parties.

Is this legal advice?

No. PrivCheckr provides informational insights and does not replace professional legal advice.

Which laws does PrivCheckr consider?

PrivCheckr focuses on principles derived from India’s Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

Who can use PrivCheckr?

Students, app users, developers, researchers, and anyone who wants to understand privacy policies clearly.

Is PrivCheckr free?

Yes. PrivCheckr is currently provided as an academic and research project.
